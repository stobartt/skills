# Primary sources and limits of transfer

Choose sources by the research question and intended medium. These are starting points, not a required reading list or a complete coverage checklist. Consult applicable current editions and the project's own evidence. The entries summarize useful principles; source artwork and files are not bundled with this skill.

| Source | Useful principle | Limit of transfer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [USWDS: Design principles](https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-principles/) | Shared principles can support continuity while implementations respond to different contexts. | Government-specific values, requirements, and identity do not become defaults for other projects. |
| [Duke: Identity](https://brand.duke.edu/duke-identity/) | When brand foundations are in scope, connect purpose and values to tone and messaging. | The institution's strategy and chapter structure are not universal requirements. |
| [IBM Design Language: 8-Bar](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/ibm-logos/8-bar/) | Optical logo variants, background selection, and clear-space rules explained through examples. | Specific proportions, colors, and legal conditions belong to IBM's identity. |
| [Morningstar Design: Family brands](https://design.morningstar.com/foundations/family-brands?tab=Usage) | A diagram can identify which parts of a lockup count toward clear space. | Morningstar excludes the trademark or service mark from this measurement for these lockups. Follow each brand's own diagram. |
| [Atlassian Design: Logos](https://atlassian.design/foundations/logos/) | Connect usage context, rules, and downloadable variants; distinguish a standalone symbol from a symbol-and-name combination. | Containers and dimensions are defined for Atlassian's identity. |
| [IBM Design Language: Color](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/color/) | A color system includes roles and permitted combinations. | The number of steps and particular hue combinations are not universal. |
| [Mailchimp: Voice and Tone](https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/voice-and-tone/) | Voice stays consistent while tone responds to the reader's situation. | A brand's personality and humor do not transfer automatically. |
| [W3C: Contrast (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) | Text contrast uses relative luminance, with distinct conditions for large text and specified exceptions. | This is one accessibility criterion, not certification of a brandbook. |
| [W3C: SVG basic shapes](https://www.w3.org/TR/SVG2/shapes.html) | SVG circles, rectangles, and other basic shapes have equivalent path geometry. | Choose the editable representation and export format from the delivery requirements. |
| [MDN: rel=icon](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Attributes/rel#icon) | Browser icon selection depends on media, type, and sizes. | Installation and platform icons have separate requirements. |
| [web.dev: Building an adaptive favicon](https://web.dev/articles/building/an-adaptive-favicon) | A favicon can respond to the environment's preferred color scheme. | The article demonstrates SVG; verify current support in the target browsers. |
| [Sharp: Resizing images](https://sharp.pixelplumbing.com/api-resize/) and [Output options](https://sharp.pixelplumbing.com/api-output/) | Controlled raster exports at chosen sizes and formats. | The library neither makes design decisions nor establishes the output's visual quality. |
| [GOV.UK: Moderated usability testing](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/using-moderated-usability-testing) | Observe representative readers completing meaningful tasks without being told the solution. | The method supports guide usability checks; it does not establish brand preference or market positioning. |

Record the project's own conclusion and how the source supports it. For an additional platform, consult its official documentation rather than inferring requirements from another product's icon.
